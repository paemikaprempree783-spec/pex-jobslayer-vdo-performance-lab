# Platform Adaptation Briefs

Use these as starting constraints only; current platform guidance and the user's placement are authoritative.

## Adaptation dimensions

For each destination, check:

- first-frame legibility in the intended crop
- sound-on and sound-off comprehension
- safe-area placement for text and CTA
- duration and the speed of the first meaningful payoff
- native-feeling format: creator, demo, testimonial, product proof, or motion graphic
- comment/share/save behavior relevant to the business job

## Variant planning

Do not simply resize one master. Preserve the persuasion mechanism while changing the opening, crop, text density, and payoff timing to fit the placement. Record which variables are controlled and which are intentionally changed.

## Evidence limitation

Platform suitability is a hypothesis unless placement, analytics, or user context confirms it. Never state that a video will perform better on a platform solely from aspect ratio or trend familiarity.
