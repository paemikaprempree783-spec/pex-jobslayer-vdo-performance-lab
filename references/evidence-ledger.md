# Evidence Ledger Reference

Use the smallest reliable claim that the asset supports. Never upgrade an inference into a fact.

| Label | Use when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| VISIBLE | Object, action, setting, expression, camera or edit is directly visible | `VISIBLE: product appears in close-up at 0:04` |
| AUDIBLE | Spoken words, music, SFX, silence, or vocal delivery is audible | `AUDIBLE: voiceover states ...` |
| ON_SCREEN | Text, logo, subtitle, price, offer, or CTA is readable | `ON_SCREEN: discount text appears at 0:12` |
| USER_SUPPLIED | User provides script, metric, brand fact, or context | `USER_SUPPLIED: purchase event is the primary KPI` |
| INFERRED | Reasoned interpretation of evidence, clearly marked | `INFERRED: demonstration may reduce trust friction` |
| UNCLEAR | Not readable, inaudible, cropped, too compressed, or unavailable | `UNCLEAR: exact spoken claim` |

## Confidence rubric

- **High:** directly observed and unambiguous.
- **Medium:** observed but partially obscured, or interpretation has one plausible alternative.
- **Low:** inference with limited support; use only as a test hypothesis.

## Claim discipline

1. Separate what happened in the video from why it may work.
2. Reference timestamps for material findings.
3. Never infer demographic or sensitive traits from appearance.
4. Do not infer conversion performance from production polish.
5. Treat any retention or CPA claim as `USER_SUPPLIED` unless analytics are supplied.
